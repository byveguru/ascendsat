Cohort 2 · Spring 2027

Eight Fridays. Idea on Friday one. Demo day on Friday eight.

Fridays, 6:00 to 8:00 PM · downtown Cary area · Free

Applications for Cohort 2 close January 8, 2027. The first Friday is January 15.

These dates are predicted. Cohort 2 runs after Cohort 1 finishes, and we will confirm the schedule before applications open. Everything below is what we are planning, not a commitment.

The eight Fridays

  • 01 Kickoff

    Friday, January 15 · 6:00 to 8:00 PM

    Kickoff: your idea, your team

    Meet the cohort, share your idea in 30 seconds, and join or form a team. Then learn how to define the need (what people want but do not have), the problem (what is stopping them), and your solution.

    You leave with A team, and a clear need, problem, and solution.

  • 02 AI toolkit

    Friday, January 22 · 6:00 to 8:00 PM

    AI toolkit

    A hands-on introduction to the AI tools you will use to build: prompting, chatbots, and no-code builders. Try them on your own idea, with mentors around to help.

    You leave with Experience using AI tools on your project.

  • 03 Networking night

    Friday, January 29 · 6:00 to 8:00 PM

    Networking night and pitch coaching

    Meet mentors, professors, and local founders. Give your first short pitch to a coach in a small group and get feedback.

    You leave with First pitch feedback, and new connections.

  • 04 Build day 1

    Friday, February 5 · 6:00 to 8:00 PM

    Build day 1

    A full session on building. The focus is getting a real first version of your idea working, and AI is the main way you will do it: generating and editing code, laying out screens, writing the copy, and standing up something people can actually click. You watch a live example of a first version being built, then start on yours with mentors going around to help.

    You leave with The start of a working version of your idea.

  • 05 Build day 2

    Friday, February 12 · 6:00 to 8:00 PM

    Build day 2 and demo practice

    The second build session. Same focus, further along: keep building and make it better, using AI to debug what broke, refine what works, and fill in the gaps. You also learn how to show it off, then practice a 60-second demo with a partner.

    You leave with A working demo you can show.

  • 06 Business basics

    Friday, February 19 · 6:00 to 8:00 PM

    Business basics and storytelling

    Learn who pays for an idea, what it costs, and how it can make money. Then turn your problem into a short story and sketch your pitch outline.

    You leave with A basic business model and a pitch outline.

  • 07 Rehearsal

    Friday, February 26 · 6:00 to 8:00 PM

    Pitch coaching and rehearsal

    Pitch your full presentation to a coach, with questions and answers, then run it on the stage with a timer and feedback from other teams. Review how judging works.

    You leave with A polished, practiced pitch.

  • 08 Demo day

    Friday, March 5 · 6:00 to 8:00 PM

    Demo day and pitch competition

    Teams pitch to a panel of judges in front of an audience, then everyone celebrates with food and an awards ceremony.

    You leave with A pitch on the big stage, and feedback from judges.

    Open to the public

Between sessions, each team has one task: interviews, a prototype, or a pitch. The sessions are workshops and feedback. The building happens in between.

Free. No cap on teams. $1,000 for first place.

  • Demo day (March 5) is open to the public. The other Fridays are for students and mentors.

Cohort 1 runs sooner and its dates are confirmed: five Fridays, November 6 to December 4, 2026. You choose which one you are applying to on the form.

Cohort 1, fall 2026. NC Ignyte is a free startup accelerator and pitch competition for North Carolina high school students. Five Fridays, November 6 to December 4, 2026, in the downtown Cary area.

Write to hello@ncignyte.org. See what our founders are building. Read the code of conduct and the privacy page. Find us on Instagram at @nc.ignyte and on LinkedIn.

Privacy: we collect only what the application asks. We do not sell student information, and we do not share it with sponsors, partners, or schools. See the privacy page for the one narrow exception, used to help draft decision letters. We email students and their parent or guardian about NC Ignyte only.
