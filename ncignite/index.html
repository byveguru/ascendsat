The questions people actually ask

Is this the first cohort?

Yes. Cohort 1 is a five-week sprint in fall 2026, five Fridays from November 6 to December 4. Cohort 2 is the full eight-week program, expected to run January 15 to March 5, 2027, with applications closing January 8. Those dates are predicted and we will confirm them before applications open. Because Cohort 1 is a fast sprint, expect demos to be early-stage: working enough to show and explain, not finished products. You will help shape the program, so tell us what works and what does not. Students who finish can apply to mentor future cohorts.

Is it really free?

Yes. No application fee, no tuition, nothing to buy. Students never pay. We are raising sponsor support to cover the space, prizes, and food.

Who can join?

Any high school student in North Carolina can apply. There is no cap on the number of teams. We accept every eligible student who completes the application and whose parent confirms. Sessions are in person in the downtown Cary area, so plan on getting there each week.

Do I need a team?

No. You can apply alone. Teams start at 1 and there is no upper limit, and you can form a team at the kickoff.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The building workshop is designed for beginners, and there are people in the room to help you.

What will we build with?

Whatever fits your idea. The building workshop covers using and building with AI alongside other ways to get a demo working. The specific tools are to be announced, they will be widely available ones, and we will publish the list before the first session so you can set up accounts in advance.

What happens at demo day?

Every team gives its final pitch and shows its demo to the judges. First place wins $1,000, with prizes for second and third. Every team gets feedback and a certificate. It is open to the public, so bring people.

What happens after demo day?

Six months after your cohort ends, we host a virtual check-in for alumni. You share an update on what you have built since, get feedback, and connect with other NC Ignyte students. It is free and open to every student who completed the program.

Is it safe, and what should my parents know?

Sessions are in person at a venue we share with families after an application is processed. We ask for a parent or guardian email on the application so we can send details straight to families. After you apply, we email your parent or guardian a confirmation code. They enter it to unlock the consent form. Once they submit it, we review your application and email you a decision. Everyone follows our code of conduct. More for parents. We collect only what the application asks and we do not share student information.

What does the application ask?

Your name, phone, and email. Your parent's name, email, and phone. Your school and grade. The name of your startup and what you are building (write "not sure yet" if you do not have one). Whether you are solo or on a team. Why you want to join and your goals. Whether you can make all five Fridays, and any food allergies. Then your parent confirms with a code we email them.

Something we did not answer? Email hello@ncignyte.org.

Why we started this

Building something in high school is mostly done alone. You can find tutorials, but not a room full of people your age trying the same thing, or a deadline that makes you finish. We wanted that room when we were starting out, so we are building it for the people coming next.
